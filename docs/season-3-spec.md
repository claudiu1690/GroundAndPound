# Season 3 (v3.0.0) design spec

Build-feature step 1 (game-designer), 2026-10-28. Scope: the four v3.0.0 items in
`docs/season-3-plan.md`. Season 3 opens automatically Fri 2026-12-04 (after the 5-day off-season that follows Season 2 on 2026-11-29) and ends Fri 2027-02-12 (70
days). Open format continues (`OPEN_SPLIT_AT_SEASON = null`). Theme: winter. Rejected and not
proposed: advent calendar, daily login rewards, anything time-limited that is not cosmetic, anything
sold that touches fight results. No em dashes or en dashes in player-facing copy.

**Name decided 2026-10-28: the owner named Season 3 "Black Ice".** The twist key becomes `black_ice` (name "Black Ice") and everything this spec calls Frostbite (twist, theme map, banner set, badges `pvp_winter_s3*`, poster lines) takes the Black Ice name.

**Status: design only. Owner answers to the open questions (marked per item) are pending. The
architect contract (step 2) comes after those answers.**

Two facts found while reading that shape the design:

- `Season.twist` is a closed enum over `TWIST_KEYS` (`models/seasonModel.js:7`), and `ROTATION_TWISTS
  = TWIST_KEYS` (`pvpSeasonService.js:20`). A new twist key MUST be appended to `TWISTS` (or the Season
  doc fails validation), and the rotation list must then be decoupled from `TWIST_KEYS` or Season 4+
  shifts.
- `the_contenders` as shipped is a no-op: `streakFrom: 3` equals `DP.STREAK_MIN`
  (`tests/services/pvpDp.test.js` says so in its title). Season 3 would have been the first season to
  ship a twist that does nothing, so replacing it is an improvement, not just a reskin.

## 1. Winter season skin + Season 3 twist

### 1.1 Goal
Make the first season that straddles the holidays feel like an event without adding power: a
winter-named twist that shifts the ladder meta away from Season 2's finish-heavy Blood Sport, and a
cold visual treatment on the surfaces where the season is already the subject. Everything flips on
with the automatic rollover and off with Season 4: no deploy on release night, no calendar logic.

### 1.2 Mechanics

**Twist: "Frostbite"** (recommended). Shape `methods + pct`, like Blood Sport / Ground War / Iron Fist
/ The Marathon, so `pvpDpService.computeDp` step 5, `twistCopyFor`, `twistApplied`, the Twist Master
badge and the FightResult twist breakdown line all work with zero engine logic.

```
frostbite: { name: "Frostbite", methods: ["submission", "decision"], pct: 0.20, seasonal: true }
```

Player-facing effect line (auto-generated by `twistCopyFor`): "+20% Division Points on
Submission/Decision wins".

Why submission + decision: Season 2 paid finishes. Frostbite pays the slow freeze, the grind to the
cards and the squeeze on the mat, so grapplers and durable decision fighters get their season and
KO-power builds do not win two seasons running on the same lever. Fallback if the owner prefers a KO
season: **"Whiteout"**, `methods: ["ko"], pct: 0.30` (a reskin of Iron Fist). Both names are clear of
Gym Wars, Irongate, Ashwood and every Special Move and badge name.

**Selection: a per-season override map, not a rotation edit.**

- `SEASON_TWIST_OVERRIDES = { 3: "frostbite" }` in `consts/pvpConfig.js`, next to `TWISTS`.
- `TWISTS.frostbite` is appended (so the Season enum accepts it) and flagged `seasonal: true`.
- `ROTATION_TWISTS = TWIST_KEYS.filter(k => !TWISTS[k].seasonal)` (still the six shipped twists, same
  indices).
- `pickTwistForSeason(n)`: `n <= 1` iron_circuit; override map next; else rotation as today.

Result: Season 3 = Frostbite; Season 4 = `(4-1) % 6 = 3` = Ground War, identical to today. Because
`teaseSeason`, `finalizeSeason`, the `softReset` fallback seed and `seedPerWcCycle` all go through
`pickTwistForSeason`, the landing countdown, the pre-season tease and the rollover cannot disagree.

**Theme derivation (single home, server side).** `SEASON_THEMES = { frostbite: "winter" }` in
`pvpConfig.js`. Every season DTO that carries `twist`/`twistName` (`GET /pvp/season/public` head and
`next`, `GET /pvp/season/current/:wc`, the dashboard `pvp` block) also carries `theme: "winter" |
null`. The frontend applies a `theme-winter` class and never looks at dates or season numbers. Gating
is "the live season's twist has a theme", not a calendar window:

- The skin turns on in the same 10-minute sweep that activates Season 3 and off when Season 4
  activates. A calendar window would end mid-season or disagree with a season that slipped.
- Staging can rehearse it by forcing the rollover, like Season 2.
- Between Nov 20 and Dec 4 the landing hero promotes `next` (14-day tease window); `next.theme ===
  "winter"` lets that tease frost up while the live Season 2 band stays as it is. **The override must
  be on production before Nov 20** or the tease advertises the old name.

**Surfaces that get the winter treatment** (all CSS, all behind `theme-winter`):

| Surface | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Proving Ground hub header (`PvpHub.jsx` season block) | Frost vignette on the header card, ice-blue twist pill, 2 to 3 slow snow layers behind the title only (not over the ladder table) |
| Pre-season countdown (`PreSeasonCountdown.jsx`) | Same header frost, snow behind the timer |
| Home hero (`AthleteHero.jsx` over the octagon image) | Cold colour grade overlay on the existing octagon image, light snow layer, frosted edge on the two banner plates. The Proving Ground tile gets the ice-blue pill only |
| Landing season hero + Proving Ground band | Snow layer and frost on the band; the live dot stays red |
| Season rollover poster (`SeasonPosterModal.jsx`) | The NOW LIVE band gets a frost trim and ice-blue accent; the results half (Season 2 CLOSED) stays untouched |

Not themed: ladder rows, fight result, DP breakdown, matchmaking cards, anything in PvE, the sidebar,
the Shop. Winter frames the season, it does not fog the numbers.

**Winter, not Christmas.** The skin runs to Feb 12, so snow, frost, ice blue and cold white with the
house red and gold kept. Christmas flourishes belong to the date-gated Holiday fight card in 3.1.

**Reduced motion and performance.** Snow is pure CSS: two or three `radial-gradient` dot tiles on
`position:absolute; inset:0; pointer-events:none; aria-hidden` layers animated with `transform:
translateY` (no canvas, no rAF, no DOM particles). Reduced motion: animation off, layers hidden or
frozen; frost vignette and colour grade stay. Mobile: one layer maximum.

**Edge cases.**
- No season data or fetch error: `theme` is null and every surface renders as today.
- A stale `upcoming` Season 3 doc on staging keeps its stored twist (`seedSeason` heals only `name` on
  a dup-key re-run). Rehearsal step: check `Season.find({ seasonNumber: 3 })` on staging before
  forcing the rollover. Prod has no upcoming doc.
- Frontend mirror `frontend/src/components/pvp/pvpConst.js` `TWISTS` needs a `frostbite` entry or the
  raw key renders.

### 1.3 Numbers
`pct: 0.20` on two methods. Base 120 win → 144 (+24 DP); with a +25% bracket bonus, 150 → 180 (+30).
Blood Sport paid +30 on a base win. Full stack under Frostbite (belt +50, rivalry +25, bracket 25%,
twist, streak) = 366 versus 381 under Blood Sport: marginally gentler by design. Catch-up and streak
untouched.

### 1.4 Season 3 poster lines (`pvp.seasonPoster.whatsNew.s3`)
Line 1 is always the auto-derived twist line. These follow it:

```
"s3": {
  "line1": { "lead": "Winter on the ladder.", "body": "The Proving Ground runs cold all season. Grind it out: submissions and decisions pay extra under Frostbite." },
  "line2": { "lead": "Frostbite banner set.", "body": "Fight once this season and the Frostbite background, the Frost accent and the snowflake badge are yours to keep, forever. Ten fights add the Long Night background." },
  "line3": { "lead": "Faces.", "body": "Choose your fighter's portrait from your Profile." }
}
```
If portraits slip, drop line3 and the default line takes over.

### 1.5 Gating
Nothing unlocks it; the Proving Ground unlock (3 career wins) is unchanged. Twist Master becomes
earnable again in Season 3.

### 1.6 Data needs (architect)
- `consts/pvpConfig.js`: `TWISTS.frostbite` (`seasonal: true`), `SEASON_TWIST_OVERRIDES`,
  `SEASON_THEMES`, exported.
- `pvpSeasonService`: rotation excludes seasonal twists; `pickTwistForSeason` checks the override map
  first; `themeFor(twistKey)` next to `twistCopyFor`.
- `theme` on `/pvp/season/public` (head and `next`), `/pvp/season/current/:weightClass`, dashboard
  `pvp` block. Display only, never persisted.
- No schema change (the enum grows with `TWIST_KEYS`).
- Tests: `tests/services/pvpSeasonTease.qa.test.js:130` asserts `TWIST_KEYS.length === 6`; change to
  rotation length 6, `pickTwistForSeason(3) === "frostbite"`, `pickTwistForSeason(4) ===
  "ground_war"`.
- i18n `s3` lines; `pvpConst.TWISTS.frostbite` mirror.

### 1.7 Player and integration impact
Grapplers and decision fighters get a season where their natural win method pays; strikers lose
nothing (base DP unchanged). Bots gain slightly more DP via submissions but `BOT_MAX_DP` still clamps
them. Landing reads the same DTO, so marketing can use "Frostbite" with no drift. Library "Seasons,
twists and rewards" (~L926) adds Frostbite; GDD §22.6 adds the override map and theme rule.

### 1.8 Open questions (owner)
1. Twist: **Frostbite** (submission + decision, +20%) or **Whiteout** (KO, +30%)? Recommend Frostbite.
2. `the_contenders` is a no-op. Fix it to `streakFrom: 2` now (it next appears in Season 9)?
3. Optional art, needs approval: one generated winter variant of the octagon hero backdrop (1 image, 2
   candidate renders). Recommend skipping for 3.0 and judging the CSS grade first.
4. `PvpHub.jsx:145` builds "Season N — Name" with an em dash. Fix to a colon while the hub is open?

## 2. Holiday banner pieces (Frostbite set)

### 2.1 Goal
A permanent, visible souvenir on the cosmetic every fighter already carries everywhere (sidebar, Home
hero, Career Profile, ladder). Earned by fighting, never sold, kept forever. Also a soft pull from
PvE-only players into the Proving Ground.

### 2.2 Mechanics

| Piece | Kind | Unlock | Visual direction (CSS only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BG_FROSTBITE` "Frostbite" | background | badge `pvp_winter_s3` | House 115deg slash in ice blue (`rgba(125,211,252,0.85)`) on navy-black (`#0b1220` to `#111318`), new `frost` texture: sparse white specks + pale vignette in the top corners |
| `ACC_FROST` "Frost" | accent | badge `pvp_winter_s3` | `#cfefff` pale ice (distinct from Ice Blue, Teal Ice, Platinum, Bone White) |
| `BADGE_FROSTBITE` "Frostbite Season" | badge (pinnable) | badge `pvp_winter_s3` | glyph `❄` |
| `BG_LONG_NIGHT` "Long Night" | background | badge `pvp_winter_s3_veteran` | Midnight blue with one faint aurora streak (teal to violet band at 100deg, low alpha), `grain` texture |

No frame piece: frames are nameplate layouts in `BannerPreview`, a component change, not a catalog
entry.

**Unlocks in player words.**
- Tier 1 (`pvp_winter_s3`, "Frostbite Season"): fight once in the Proving Ground as the attacker during
  Season 3. Placement bouts count. Win or lose.
- Tier 2 (`pvp_winter_s3_veteran`, "Long Night"): reach 10 fights on your Season 3 record (`wins +
  losses`), attacks and defenses both counted.

**Where the state lives.** `bannerService.isUnlocked` reads fighter state only, and PVPRecord is not on
the fighter, so the unlock reads a badge in `fighter.badgesEarned`. Two fixed descriptors in
`PVP_BADGE_DEFS` (fixed defs are consulted before the `pvp_<division>_s<N>` regex):

```
pvp_winter_s3:         { name: "Frostbite Season", description: "Fought in the Proving Ground during Season 3, the Frostbite season.", icon: "Snowflake", color: "#7DD3FC", category: "proving_ground" }
pvp_winter_s3_veteran: { name: "Long Night",       description: "Fought 10 times in the Proving Ground during Season 3.",            icon: "Moon",      color: "#7DD3FC", category: "proving_ground" }
```

Minted in `evaluatePvpFightBadges` (per-fight, idempotent via `awardBadge`), gated on the fight's
season being Season 3 (`seasonCtx.seasonNumber === 3` or `twist === "frostbite"`).

**Mint at first fight, not at finalize:** the piece is usable while the season runs; finalize only
reaches players with a record and division badges exist only for Challenger and up; the per-fight hook
already exists. Optional finalize safety net: any Season 3 record with at least one fight and no Tier
1 badge gets it (covers defense-only players).

**Edge cases.**
- After Season 4 activates nothing can mint either badge, so the set is a true Season 3 marker. Pieces
  stay in the catalog, locked for those who missed it, with an unlock hint.
- Bots never evaluate badges for themselves, so no bot wears the set; that is not a tell.
- PvP fights do not run the PvE "Banner Unlocked" diff. Nice-to-have: one line on the PvP FightResult
  when a tier badge was minted. Otherwise the pieces light up in the editor (as Throne Room does).
- `describeUnlock` prints `Badge: <raw id>`; add an optional `unlockHint` per piece ("Fight once in
  Season 3", "10 Proving Ground fights in Season 3").
- Frontend mirror `frontend/src/components/banner/bannerCatalog.js` needs the four pieces and
  `BannerPreview` a `bp2-tex--frost` class.

### 2.3 Numbers
1 fight for three pieces (a hook, not a grind; one 15-energy attack). 10 fights for the fourth: 70 days
at the decay-free cadence of one fight a week is exactly 10. No DP, cash, fame or energy attached.
Catalog goes 12 → 14 backgrounds, 11 → 12 accents, 13 → 14 badges.

### 2.4 Data needs (architect)
Two fixed ids in `consts/pvpBadges.js`; a season field in `pvpCtx`; confirm the hook runs for placement
bouts and that the defender path has the updated record for Tier 2; four catalog entries + frontend
mirror; `frost` texture; optional `unlockHint` in `describeUnlock`; optional finalize safety net. No
schema change.

### 2.5 Integration impact
Two new Proving Ground badges in the Career Profile grid; feed already logs badge events. GDD §20.3
counts and §22.12, Library "Banner Customizer" and "Proving Ground badges", changelog `3.0`.

### 2.6 Open questions (owner)
1. Tier 2 at 10 fights with defenses counted, or attacks only?
2. PvP FightResult one-line "banner piece unlocked" notice in 3.0, or leave discovery to the editor?

## 4. Kicks honesty fix (no engine change)

### 4.1 What is true today
- `LEG` is read in three places in `utils/fightResolution.js`: `strikeDamage` (`(STR + LEG) / 2`),
  `strikingComposite` (`(STR + SPD + LEG) / 3`, sets the striker/grappler profile) and
  `playerShootsTakedown` (same composite). No leg-kick stamina drain, no head-kick KO roll. Kicks in the
  fight text are style narration.
- Library LEG row (`libraryContent.js:106`) promises both. False.
- `"Leg Kick Attrition"` is in `FIGHT_STRATEGIES` and stamped on every Muay Thai opponent by
  `STYLE_STRATEGY`. The engine's strategy branches do not recognise it, so it equals no strategy. No
  client component renders it.

### 4.2 Corrected Library copy (replaces L106)
> **LEG, Leg Power.** Kicking power. Averaged with STR to set how hard your strikes land, and part of
> your striking profile (STR, SPD, LEG) that decides whether you fight on the feet or look for the
> takedown. There is no separate leg-kick or head-kick mechanic: the kicks in the fight text are your
> style's flavour, not a hidden stat effect.

No "coming soon" in player copy; the Season 4 kick mechanic is announced when it ships.

### 4.3 Leg Kick Attrition: remove (recommended)
A "small real effect" would mean mapping Muay Thai opponents to an existing literal (Pressure Fighter =
+10% strike damage), a real mid-season balance change to an eighth of the PvE pool on the most lopsided
striking style. That belongs in the Season 4 work with sweeps.

Removal with zero behaviour change and no prod migration:
- Delete `'Leg Kick Attrition'` from `FIGHT_STRATEGIES` (`consts/gameConstants.js`).
- `STYLE_STRATEGY['Muay Thai']` becomes explicit `null`; `strategyForStyle` returns `null` for an
  explicit null entry (today any falsy value falls back to Pressure Fighter, which would be the balance
  change in disguise).
- `models/opponentModel.js` `strategy` enum becomes `[...Object.keys(FIGHT_STRATEGIES), 'Leg Kick
  Attrition']` (legacy-tolerant, like `GAMEPLAN_KEYS_WITH_LEGACY`) so stored values keep validating.
  Reseeds converge to null whenever they next run; no dedicated prod run.
- Balance scripts keep producing the same Muay Thai numbers (null and the unknown literal are the same
  branch). Verify with a local, no-DB `balanceSweep.js` run before and after.

### 4.4 Impact
Mechanically nothing. The Library stops promising effects that never existed, so the Season 4 kick
mechanic reads as new. Changelog `3.0` Changed line: "The Library's LEG line now says what LEG actually
does. Kicks in the fight text are style flavour; there is no hidden leg-kick stamina drain or head-kick
KO roll, and there never was."

### 4.5 Open question (owner)
Confirm **remove** over **real effect**.
