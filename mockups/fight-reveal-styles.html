Fight replay: six ways to show it

The same 5-round debut (you win by rear-naked choke in round 5) shown six ways. The problem with the current replay: it is a stream of sentences, so it is either too fast to read or too slow to enjoy. Every style below moves the information out of sentences and into things you see at a glance (bars, positions, numbers, one or two words), and keeps full sentences for the one or two moments per round that earn them, held long enough to read.
