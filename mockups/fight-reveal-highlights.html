Highlights, with your cards

Style A from the six, plus what you asked for: your special moves and your camp are part of the show. Same 5-round debut. You have three cards equipped and two camp sessions booked.

What you're looking at

  • Your corner (the strip under the stats): your equipped cards and camp sessions sit there dimmed from the first second. When one fires it lights up, and a small counter shows how many times. You can see at a glance what worked.
  • A signature card firing (Overhand Right, round 4) is the biggest moment in the fight: the painted card slams in with its rarity glow, a flash and a shake, then the line that says what it did.
  • A proc card firing (Sprawl, Full Mount) slams in the same way, a little smaller, with the condition that triggered it.
  • A passive card (Heavy Hands, always on) never interrupts: it shows once as a small pill near the top and stays lit in your corner.
  • Camp gets a gold card with the session name and what it did. In round 2 your camp session and your Sprawl card fire on the same takedown: both show, back to back, as one combo.
  • Their wildcard (the hidden tendency from the Fighter Report) gets a red striped card when it fires, so you learn what you didn't prepare for.
  • The result lists everything that fired and how often, plus anything that never fired (dimmed), which tells you what to change for the next camp.

What it needs in the game

  • Camp moments already exist in the fight log (camp_*_fired) and the wildcard too. They only need the new cards.
  • Special moves are tracked per fight (fired yes or no, and how many times) but not when. The engine needs to write a "move fired" moment into the fight log, the same way camp does. That is a backend change; the fight maths stay the same.
  • Short commentary lines (8 words or so) for the highlight cards, since today's lines were written to be read in a list.
