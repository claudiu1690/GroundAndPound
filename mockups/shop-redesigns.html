Shop redesign · three directions

Every product, price and effect below is the real one from the game's config. Direction A has the generated product art for every item (Energy Drink can, Energy Shot, 7 boosters, 7 supplements). B and C still show CSS stand-ins for the Cash items.

Premium (real money) is the Energy Drink bundles only: 6 for $4.99, 15 for $9.99, 40 for $19.99, 100 for $39.99. Each direction gives them the emphasis a different way.

APremium up front, then quiet

Reworked after the game designer's review. Premium is shown in full once (the first Shop visit, and once after a guest secures their account), then folds to a single line. The free ways to earn drinks sit inside the panel, and the strongest honest moment to offer a drink is when an action fails for lack of energy, with the free and Cash options listed first.
